Curve Layers

    Each layer header is editable — rename it and the Milestones panel updates to match. Use the Layer dropdown below to move a curve between layers.

    Theme

    Themes recolor curves by their color role (set per curve below). Tweak any colors, then Save as theme to reuse them later.

    Visual Title

    The title text is the visual name (top-left). Drag it on the canvas to position it — it obeys Snap and exports with the chart.

      Branch

      A branch marks a handoff where the story forks into separate tracks — the node, divider and tint highlight where one layer gives way to the next. Needs at least two layers; with a single layer it's off and curves span the whole canvas.
